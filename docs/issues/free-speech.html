What the issue is

Coming soon

This section will explain the First Amendment idea in everyday words: the government generally may not jail or fine people for criticizing it. It will also say that “protected from the government” is not the same thing as “protected from every consequence,” such as a private company or a school with its own rules.

No statistics will be added until they are checked against primary sources.
